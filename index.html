Intelligence is easy to imagine in the abstract. It becomes tangible in a hand closing around a cup, a careful reach, a folded piece of fabric, or the rhythm of a running body.

These small moments are full of coordination. Joints move together. A grip adjusts. Balance shifts. What looks effortless is a conversation between a machine and the world around it.

FIELDWORK is a collection of motion studies exploring that conversation. Four everyday gestures, made visible one character at a time.
