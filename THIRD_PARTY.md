## Franka Emika Panda visual meshes

The Three.js trajectory and Viser examples share the complete Panda visual model from [Google DeepMind MuJoCo Menagerie](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/0059d4335f8156206f63a35662313385f7ad6d74/franka_emika_panda), derived upstream from Franka Emika `franka_ros`. License: **Apache-2.0**, retained at `public/models/panda/LICENSE` with source attribution at `public/models/panda/NOTICE.md`.

Pinned commit: `0059d4335f8156206f63a35662313385f7ad6d74`. The original visual OBJ meshes are converted without decimation into per-link GLB, retaining their MJCF material colors. `public/models/panda/source.json` records geometry counts, conversion details, and SHA-256 hashes; `panda.xml` preserves the upstream kinematics. Rebuild with `scripts/build-panda-assets.py` using NumPy and Trimesh. The shared authored motion is generated by `scripts/build-panda-trajectory.py`.
